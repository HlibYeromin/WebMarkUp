Traditions

  • Golden Autumn Festival

    The annual youth festival "Golden Autumn of Slavutych" (known locally as ZOSYA) is one of the most vibrant and energetic events of the year. It brings together creative young people, journalists, and media professionals from across the country.

    During these days, the city is filled with incredible energy, creative projects, street concerts, and joyful flash mobs, such as mass dry-paint throwing in the central square.

  • City Day

    The large-scale City Day celebration traditionally brings together all residents and visitors to Slavutych. This spectacular event highlights the city's unity and multicultural heritage.

    The central streets and squares become the main venue for the festivities: this is where ceremonial parades take place, with residents carrying huge flags, accompanied by live music and fairs.

  • Night of Remembrance

    Despite its vitality, Slavutych cherishes and deeply honors the history of its founding. Every year, on the night of April 26, residents gather at the memorial to the victims of the Chernobyl disaster.

    In the silence, illuminated only by the light of hundreds of lit candles and oil lamps, the city pays tribute to the firefighters, cleanup workers, and power plant employees whose selflessness gave life to this new city.

Did you know?

The name ZOSYA comes from the first letters of the Ukrainian name of the festival: Zolota Osin Slavutycha, which means "Golden Autumn of Slavutych".
