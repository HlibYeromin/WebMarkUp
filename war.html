War in Slavutych

  • Isolation And Threat

    At the start of the full-scale invasion in 2022, Slavutych found itself in an extremely difficult situation. Due to its location near the border and the Chernobyl Exclusion Zone, the city was quickly cut off from its main supply routes.

    Soon, armed forces and enemy armored vehicles appeared on the streets, attempting to establish physical control over the territory and administrative buildings.

  • Peaceful Resistance

    Despite the direct military threat and the lack of regular electricity and communications, the residents of Slavutych demonstrated incredible courage.

    When troops attempted to occupy the city, thousands of residents took to the main streets. They organized a large-scale peaceful protest, unfurling a huge Ukrainian flag. The people stood as a united human shield, chanting patriotic slogans and demanding that the armed soldiers leave their home.

  • Alley of Heroes

    The fighters who died defending their country's freedom are revered in Slavutych. There are special memorial exhibits that feature portraits and names of those who died fighting for the freedom of Ukraine.

    This has become a very significant tribute to those who have fallen fighting in defense of their land; locals, including many youths, visit the exhibit from time to time, bringing fresh flowers in tribute to their compatriots.

Remember

On March 26, 2022 thousands of residents gathered in the central square of Slavutych with Ukrainian flags. Their peaceful protest became a symbol of the courage of the whole city.
